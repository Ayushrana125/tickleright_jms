# 01. What is TickleTrail?
### Vision, Purpose & The Problem We Solve

---

## 🎯 1. Executive Summary

**TickleTrail** (internally known as the **Journey Management System / JMS**) is Tickle Right's proprietary platform for designing, automating, and monitoring personalized communication and pedagogical pathways for families enrolled in right-brain education programs.

Right-brain education is not a passive weekly tutoring session; it is a **continuous daily habit** of sensory stimulation, eidetic memory games, and parent-child bonding. TickleTrail bridges the gap between weekly physical classroom sessions and daily home habit formation.

```
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                             TICKLETRAIL PLATFORM                               |
|                                                                                |
|   Weekly Classroom Class             Between-Class Home Routine                |
|   (Physical Mat / Centre)  =====>   (Omnichannel Habit Reinforcement)          |
|                                                                                |
|     [ WhatsApp ]       [ App Push ]       [ Counsellor Call ]      [ Gifts ]   |
|     Milestone Tips     2-Min Sparks       48h Welcome SOP         Badges & Kits|
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

---

## 🛑 2. The Problems Solved

Before TickleTrail, communication with parents suffered from common edtech/offline centre pain points:

1. **Scattered Channels with No Unified Timeline:**
   * Trainers sent ad-hoc messages on personal WhatsApp groups.
   * Counsellors kept manual notes on who to call for renewals or attendance issues.
   * The Tickle Right mobile app had scheduled notifications that were completely disconnected from CRM data.
2. **Parent Fatigue & Notification Overload:**
   * If a parent was enrolled in multiple programs or had two children, they could receive 4-5 uncoordinated messages on a single morning, causing parents to mute or block numbers.
3. **No Safety Guardrails:**
   * Messages were frequently sent on Sunday mornings or national festivals, disturbing family time and lowering parent satisfaction.
4. **The "Between-Class Drop-off":**
   * Early childhood right-brain gains depend on rapid daily reinforcement (flashcard repetition, theta-state bedtime stories). Without guided daily prompts, parents forgot to practice at home, progress slowed down, and renewals dropped.

---

## 💡 3. The TickleTrail Solution

TickleTrail transforms isolated messages into **cohesive visual journeys**:

### A. True Omnichannel Touchpoints
Rather than being limited to just WhatsApp or Email, every journey step can orchestrate:
* 🟢 **WhatsApp:** Rich templates, brochures, PDF activity guides.
* 📱 **App Notifications (Push & In-App):** Direct lock-screen activity alerts routing into the mobile app via custom URI deep links (`tr://activity/...`, `tr://stories/...`).
* 📞 **Phone Calls (Human SOPs):** Structured calling tasks routed to counsellors with talking points and outcome logging.
* ✉️ **Emails:** Formal progress cards, curriculum milestones, and certificates.
* 🎁 **Physical Milestone Packs:** Logistics triggers for graduation certificates, learning kits, and habit badges.

### B. Intelligent Autopilot Guardrails
Every node in TickleTrail automatically enforces operational safety:
* **Avoid Sundays:** Touchpoints automatically roll forward to Monday morning.
* **Festive Guard:** Holds delivery during national or state holiday calendars.
* **Content Guard / Frequency Capping:** Prevents more than 2 touches per family in any 24-hour window.

### C. Unified Parent Lifecycle
From the day a parent inquires about a trial class to graduation and alumni loops, TickleTrail powers all 6 lifecycle stages:
1. **Cold Lead Nurture:** Trial invitations, curriculum video previews, follow-up calls.
2. **Member Onboarding & Companion Loop:** Daily micro-game prompts, habit building, counsellor check-ins.
3. **Active Member Retention:** Weekly progress updates, trainer feedback loops.
4. **Renewal Due (30-Day Window):** Timed milestone showcases, early renewal perks.
5. **Discontinued Win-Back:** Gentle pause periods, feedback surveys, event invites.
6. **Graduate Alumni Loop:** Advanced home practice resources, community meetups.
